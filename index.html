How to play

Complete every objective on the list, then make it to the exit. The more you finish, the more of them come.

  • Your flashlight makes you easy to see. Turn it off to sneak. Running is loud.
  • If you're spotted, break line of sight and hide in a bush, trash can, locker, wardrobe or barrel. If one sees you climb in, it will pull you out.
  • Sense points you toward the nearest objective. It needs time to recharge.
  • Map: on Easy you see the whole layout. On Hard it only shows places you've been. Task spots appear once you've found them. The game keeps running while it's open.
  • Easy: 3 lives, and your progress is kept when you're caught. Hard: one catch and it's over.
